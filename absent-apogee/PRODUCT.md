# Somos Loja

Cooperative store in Loja, Ecuador, selling products from about 300 local entrepreneurs and producers. Surface mode: Persuade.

- Goal: make the store known and bring people to it, starting with the event Loja 100 % Café Especial.
- Voice: warm, proud, plain, a little rebellious. Rejects copied concepts such as the coffee passport.
- Truth rule: real business. Never invent facts; missing data stays pending.
- Thread: the coffee process, finca to cup, ending in the store. Everything visual, little reading.
