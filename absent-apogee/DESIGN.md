# Somos Loja, vintage

Should taste of Loja: an old print of the Cathedral and Parque Central in indigo ink and watercolor on cream paper. The store is the destination and the only dark surface.

- Palette (provisional): paper #F6EEDD, indigo #1B2F6B, ochre #E0A93B, coffee #5A331C.
- Type: Yellowtail for the signature, Cormorant Garamond for headlines, Manrope for reading.
- Motion: the name is written by hand beside the living Cathedral; the square gives way to one bean; a coffee ribbon links the process; 3D beans form the 50; products tilt toward the pointer; real products pass in a carousel.
- Illustrations are AI generated from reference photos and are not documentary. Store and product photos are real.
